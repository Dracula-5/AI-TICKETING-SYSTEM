## Duplicates (P4.3 / P4.3b)

Source: `reports/duplicates/p4_duplicate_threshold.json`.

At the production threshold 0.75 (MiniLM cosine), real CQADupStack duplicates are flagged with precision 0.47 but recall only 0.18: most real duplicates are phrased too differently to clear a threshold that keeps false flags acceptable. Planted synthetic resubmissions are caught almost perfectly (precision 0.98, recall 0.99) — the synthetic check overstates real-world performance and is not used for claims.
